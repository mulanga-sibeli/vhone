AMBER / 01 — RECTANGULAR, OPENABLE BATTERY LAMP
Revision B | 15 November 2026 | All dimensions in millimetres

DESIGN
Inspired by examples/cosmos_723682210.webp: a tall amber translucent rectangular
block, clean vertical faces and a matching top cap. The photograph has no scale;
all dimensions below are proposed, not measurements of the reference.
User preference: amber translucent plastic; fabrication by a print shop.

The enclosure is a single open-top printed cup with an integral bottom. The lid
lifts straight off. A removable tray accepts interchangeable lamp-sizing rings.
There are no glued closures, magnets, screws, electrical contacts or wired
sockets. The lid and tray are gravity-retained; carry the lamp by the body/base,
never by its lid. This is a prototype design, not a tested electrical product.

PRINTING
- Suggested process: FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers. Use dry PETG and the
  filament maker's temperature/profile, calibrated by the print shop.
- Body walls: fill the full 5.0 mm section using enough perimeters and/or solid
  infill. Do NOT use vase mode or sparse infill in the translucent shell.
  Preserve the solid bottom, raised lettering and all ventilation slots.
- Make the thin plates solid; avoid sparse infill patterns visible through the
  lid. The CAD cavities are air, not volumes to fill with support or infill.
- Start with an approximately 8 mm outside brim on the tall body. A 256 mm-class
  machine accommodates the 250 mm body height and brim footprint. A larger shop
  printer is fine; no need to scale the design.
- No structural supports intended. Intake slot roofs bridge only 3 mm. The
  embossed lettering slopes upward as it projects outward, keeping its underside
  at least 45 degrees from horizontal. Inspect the sliced text and bridging.
  Print the lid inverted as supplied.
- Keep the seam on a rear corner. Match body and lid material batch; both now
  have 5 mm optical thickness. The 6.2 mm total section through the raised letters
  will transmit differently. Compared with Revision A, this thicker amber shell
  is heavier and likely dimmer; approve a lit 5 mm material sample first.
- A 0.4 mm mating gap is a nominal sliding clearance. First-layer spread can
  tighten the rings' bores: compensate/deburr and dry-fit before the full print.
  Do not force the thin shell over an oversized lid or tray.
- Geometric solid volume: approximately 917.0 cm3 for body + lid + tray;
  933.0 cm3 with the 60 mm ring. At an ASSUMED PETG density of 1.27 g/cm3 this is
  approximately 1165 g or 1185 g respectively, excluding brim, purge and waste.
  The print shop's slicer and actual filament density determine the quote.
- No physical print, slicing time estimate or dimensional calibration was done.

ASSEMBLY AND SERVICING
1. Remove brim and burrs. Confirm lid and tray slide freely without flexing walls.
2. Thread a 10 mm-wide ribbon through the two tray slots, forming a loose loop
   that can be reached just under the top opening. About 650-750 mm ribbon is a
   useful starting length. Knot on top; keep it away from the light and vents.
   The ribbon beneath the tray may raise it by its thickness; allow up to 1 mm.
3. Place the tray inside the cup. Fit one adapter if needed. Charge the complete
   lamp outside the enclosure, switch it on and lower it into the cradle.
4. Check that its switch, hook and body clear the insert and that it cannot tip
   against a wall. A tapered or unusual foot may need a custom sizing ring.
5. Set the lid in place; the locating skirt enters the cup and the four pads
   leave a deliberate 1 mm ventilation/shadow gap. Nothing locks or snaps.
6. To swap or charge, lift the lid and lift the light out. To replace the whole
   holder, use the ribbon. No adhesive or tools are needed for normal access.

USE LIMITS AND PROTOTYPE VALIDATION
Use an intact, protected, self-contained battery LED light. No mains wiring,
incandescent or halogen bulb, exposed cell or homemade battery pack is included.
The 5 W catalogue ratings above are NOT a certified enclosure power rating.
Use the lowest useful setting. Confirm with the light supplier whether another
shade is allowed; do not enclose a unit whose instructions prohibit it.
Charge outside the enclosure. Indoor, dry use only: the complete lamp has no IP
rating even if the purchased inner light does. Keep the air paths unobstructed.
Before routine use, conduct an attended full-duration trial with the actual lamp
and print, measuring temperatures against their manufacturers' limits. Stop if
there is distortion, unusual heating or smell. The CAD tests do not establish
thermal, battery, electrical, drop or tip-over safety, runtime, brightness or
remote-control performance. Approve the physical sample before repeated use.

VERIFICATION RECORD
All six printable solid models passed the text-to-cad FDM/PETG checks at 0.4 mm
nozzle, 0.2 mm layers and an assumed 256 x 256 x 256 build volume, with zero
errors and zero warnings in the final revision. These are general-practice
rules, not a print shop's qualification. Ring bores retain an informational
first-layer-spread note; dry fit/deburr them.
Exact assembly check: no shared volume among the four assembled printed parts.
Measured geometry checks: overall dimensions, 5 mm walls/bottom/lid plate, 0.4 mm lid/tray gaps,
seating contacts and the 60 x 145 cylindrical light envelope. Deliberately shifting
the lid and oversizing the light produced interference, as expected.
The script separately checks the larger 100 x 150 envelope and alternate rings.
Not all FDM rules have thresholds: pin aspect, cantilever length, generic
horizontal-hole diameter, powder/drain escape dimensions, hollow walls,
engraving dimensions, warp length and section thickness were not evaluated by
those generic rules. Thermal behaviour, optical appearance, tolerance stack-up,
residual stress, adhesion and physical handling need a real prototype.
Exported binary STLs also passed closed-edge, consistent winding, positive-volume,
CAD dimension and print-bed orientation checks. Per-part JSON reports and the
geometry/interference/STL logs are included in docs/.
Measured five front letter faces: 78.057 x 15.127 mm, centred at X=0, Z=125;
1.2 mm outward projection. Technical snapshots show the actual geometry. The appearance render uses an
illustrative emitter and scalar material; it omits individual printed layer
texture and does not predict a specific purchased light's output.
