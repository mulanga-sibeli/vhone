AMBER / 01 — RECTANGULAR, OPENABLE BATTERY LAMP
Revision C | 15 November 2026 | All dimensions in millimetres

DESIGN
Inspired by https://www.cosmos.so/e/723682210: a tall amber translucent rectangular
block, clean vertical faces and a matching top cap. The photograph has no scale;
all dimensions below are proposed, not measurements of the reference.
User preference: amber translucent plastic; fabrication by a print shop.

The enclosure is a single open-top printed cup with an integral flat bottom.
The lift-off lid is gravity-retained. A purchased E27 holder is glued centrally
onto the inside floor and supports a self-contained screw-in battery LED lamp.
The holder is unpowered; there is no mains wiring in this design. Carry by the
body/base, never by the lid. Only the enclosure and lid are printed.

HOLDER FIT AND LIMITS
The exact CAD overlap check uses a square 77 x 77 x 40 envelope resting on the
floor, centred at X=Y=0. It clears the body and lid. A 77 mm square vertical
sweep also clears the open enclosure, checking the complete insertion path.
These dimensions come from the user, not a vendor drawing. E27 identifies the
lamp interface; this check does not establish a universal holder outer size.
No separate width was provided. Confirm the actual holder, including protrusions,
is contained in the checked envelope before gluing it in place.

Adhesive thickness raises the holder and reduces headroom by the same amount.
The new battery lamp has not been identified or dimensioned, so its fit is NOT
verified. Measure its width and installed height above the holder; include
switches and other protrusions. The 206 mm central headroom is geometric space,
not a thermal clearance or recommended lamp size. Near the top edges the skirt
extends down to Z=243 and has a nominal 125.2 mm internal opening.

AMBER MATERIAL AND PRINT-SHOP BRIEF
Specify AMBER TRANSLUCENT PETG, not solid orange, opaque brown or metallic silk.

optical clarity. Request a 5.0 mm wall swatch lit by the selected lamp before the
full print. Exact colour and light transmission require a physical sample.

PRINTING
- Suggested process: FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers. Use dry PETG and the
  filament maker's temperature/profile, calibrated by the print shop.
- Body walls: fill the full 5.0 mm section using enough perimeters and/or solid
  infill. Do NOT use vase mode or sparse infill in the translucent shell.
  Preserve the solid bottom, raised lettering and all ventilation slots.
- Make the thin plates solid; avoid sparse infill patterns visible through the
  lid. The CAD cavities are air, not volumes to fill with support or infill.
- Start with an approximately 8 mm outside brim on the tall body. A 256 mm-class
  machine accommodates the 250 mm body height and brim footprint. A larger shop
  printer is fine; no need to scale the design.
- No structural supports intended. Intake slot roofs bridge only 3 mm. The
  embossed lettering slopes upward as it projects outward, keeping its underside
  at least 45 degrees from horizontal. Inspect the sliced text and bridging.
  Print the lid inverted as supplied.
- Keep the seam on a rear corner. Match body and lid material batch; both now
  have 5 mm optical thickness. The 6.2 mm total section through the raised letters
  will transmit differently. Compared with Revision A, this thicker amber shell
  is heavier and likely dimmer; approve a lit 5 mm material sample first.
- A 0.4 mm lid mating gap is a nominal sliding clearance. Compensate for
  first-layer spread/deburr and dry-fit; do not force an oversized lid.
- Use the current model_manifest.json solid volumes and the print shop's slicer
  with actual filament density for a material quote; allow brim, purge and waste.
- No physical print, slicing time estimate or dimensional calibration was done.

ASSEMBLY AND SERVICING
1. Remove brim and burrs. Confirm the lid slides freely without flexing walls.
2. Dry-fit the purchased holder centrally on the integral flat floor. Confirm
   its full footprint and the installed bulb clear the walls, lid and skirt.
3. Bond the holder to the floor using an adhesive suitable for both the printed
   PETG and the actual holder material, following its preparation and cure
   instructions. Adhesive choice and bond strength have not been validated.
4. Once cured, screw in the self-contained battery LED lamp. The bonded holder
   must resist the twisting force used to fit and remove the lamp.
5. Set the lid in place. Its four pads leave a 1 mm ventilation/shadow gap.
6. Lift the lid and unscrew the battery lamp for charging or replacement.

USE LIMITS AND PROTOTYPE VALIDATION
Use an intact, protected, self-contained battery LED light. No mains wiring,
incandescent or halogen bulb, exposed cell or homemade battery pack is included.
No enclosure power rating has been established for the selected lamp.
Use the lowest useful setting. Confirm with the light supplier whether another
shade is allowed; do not enclose a unit whose instructions prohibit it.
Charge outside the enclosure. Indoor, dry use only: the complete lamp has no IP
rating even if the purchased inner light does. Keep the air paths unobstructed.
Before routine use, conduct an attended full-duration trial with the actual lamp
and print, measuring temperatures against their manufacturers' limits. Stop if
there is distortion, unusual heating or smell. The CAD tests do not establish
thermal, battery, electrical, drop or tip-over safety, runtime, brightness or
remote-control performance. Approve the physical sample before repeated use.

VERIFICATION RECORD
Revision C checks the two printable parts and the holder envelope. Exact CAD
checks measure the 5 mm walls/bottom/lid plate, 0.4 mm lid side clearance,
26.5 mm holder side clearance, floor contact and 206 mm central headroom.
A continuous holder insertion sweep clears the open cup. Negative controls
shift the lid, shift the holder into a wall, and sink the holder through the
floor; all must report interference. The new bulb itself is not checked.

FDM checks use PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers and an assumed 256 x 256 x 256
build volume. These are general-practice rules, not a print shop qualification.
See verification_summary.json and dfm_enclosure.json / dfm_lid.json for current
status, findings and rules without applicable thresholds. Adhesive strength,
thermal behaviour, tolerance stack-up and handling require a physical prototype.

Exported STLs are checked for closed edges, consistent winding, positive volume,
CAD dimensions and print-bed orientation. The assembled STEP is checked for
interference and visually reviewed. Evidence is retained in docs/.
